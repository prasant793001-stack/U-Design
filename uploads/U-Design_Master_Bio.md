## 🔥 THE ORIGIN STORY *(long version — your evergreen "About Us")*

**Some brands are born in boardrooms. Ours was born in a mall changing room.**

Somewhere between "this shirt is nice, but why does 8,000 other guys own the same one?" and looking at a price tag that could feed a small country — U-Design was born.

The story starts with **Prashant**, a Meghalaya boy who packed his dreams (and his mom's cooking) into a suitcase, moved to Al Ain years ago, and got hit with a very specific pain: he wanted to wear clothes that felt like *him*. Not a mass-produced logo. Not the same graphic tee everyone at Al Jimi Mall was wearing. Something he actually designed. Something with a story.

So he did what every underfunded creative does — he Googled "custom t-shirt printing near me." And nearly fainted.

> *"Bro. For one t-shirt. ONE."* — Prashant, holding his phone in disbelief, 2024

And the ones that were *affordable*? The textile felt like a mosquito net that had lost hope. Wear it once, wash it once, throw it away twice.

Enter **Umair** — the Pakistani work-mate who had been listening to Prashant complain about this for approximately six months straight. Umair is the type of friend who says, *"okay habibi, less talking, more doing."* Two work colleagues. One frustrated dreamer, one calm doer. Different countries, same city, same idea: **if nobody's doing it right, WE will.**

They pooled money like two university students planning a road trip, bought a DTF printer, a sublimation printer, a heat press and a small logo press, cleared a corner of a room in Al Ain, and named the brand after the one philosophy they refused to compromise on:

# **U DESIGN. WE PRINT.**

Because here's the thing they noticed — when you *design something yourself*, you don't throw it away. You fold it. You show it off. You wear it on the good days. When something has a piece of you in it, you take care of it. And that's the exact opposite of fast fashion's whole vibe.

**U-Design is not a printing shop. It's a permission slip.**

Permission to wear the joke only your family gets. Permission to put your grandma's handwriting on a mug. Permission to make matching hoodies for your squad. Permission to gift someone something they'll actually keep. Permission to be the reason someone in Al Ain gets stopped and asked, *"bro, where did you get that shirt?"*

We print on t-shirts, mugs, keychains, and honestly — anything that stands still long enough for our heat press to find it. DTF for the vibrant stuff. Sublimation for the smooth stuff. Same-day delivery in Al Ain (because Dubai printers making you wait 5 days is a crime).

Follow along — Prashant and Umair are documenting the whole journey. The wins, the ink stains, the 2 AM print runs, the customers who make us tear up a little, the day the printer definitely tried to fight back. This isn't just a business page — it's a two-guys-figuring-it-out show. Reels, stories, YouTube shorts, behind-the-scenes chaos: all of it, all here.

Al Ain, this is *your* brand. We just run the machines.

**U DESIGN. WE PRINT.** 🎨🔥
📍 Al Ain, UAE • 📩 DM to start your design
